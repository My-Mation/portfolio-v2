COMPETITIVE HACKATHON — AVEENIR 🏆 1ST PRIZE WINNER TEAM PROJECT

1ST PRIZE
AVEENIR HACKATHON.

PROJECT BUILT: OSAMU (ADAPTIVE EXAMINATION HARDWARE SYSTEM) TIME BIND: BUILT IN 48 HOURS FROM SCRATCH HARDWARE: ESP32 MICROCONTROLLER TERMINALS · RF PACKET ENCRYPTION OUTCOME: 🏆 1ST PLACE OVERALL WINNER

In a high-intensity 48-hour sprint, we conceptualized, wired, coded, and demonstrated OSAMU — an un-cheatable exam system that abandons fragile browser locks in favor of isolated physical hardware terminals.

The system takes student exam questions directly from teacher PDFs, parses them locally, encrypts packets, and broadcasts them across offline RF channels to individual ESP32 keypad devices. A real-time adaptive engine measures student response time and question difficulty, triggering probing deep-dive questions if suspicion or hesitation is detected.

COMPETITIVE HACKATHON — PREDICTIVE HARDWARE MAINTENANCE HACKATHON BUILD TEAM PROJECT

VISO / FSOCIETY
PREDICTIVE HEALTH.

REPOSITORY: FSOCIETY (GITHUB) DISCIPLINE: EMBEDDED TELEMETRY · ACOUSTIC SOUND · VIBRATION ANALYSIS TYPE: 48-HOUR HACKATHON BUILD

Built at a rapid competitive hackathon: VISO is an industrial IoT predictive maintenance system designed around the core principle: "Machines speak before they break."

Instead of waiting for catastrophic mechanical failure, VISO continuously polls 4 independent sensor channels: high-frequency MPU6050 accelerometer vibrations, 24-band acoustic FFT audio spectrum, MQ-2 smoke/gas density, and surface thermocouple sensors. An edge anomaly model computes failure probability before physical breakdown.
