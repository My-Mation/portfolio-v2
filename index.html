03 / Field notes

I KEEP FINDING
THINGS TO BUILD.

Games led to apps. Apps led to backend. Backend led to Linux, networking, self-hosting, local AI, and eventually robots. The common thread is not a stack. It is an insistence that the thing works.

MAKE
IT
WORK.
the whole philosophy, honestly

I care about the moment software stops being a screenshot and starts touching the world: a relay clicking, a servo holding a gait, a waveform updating at sixty frames a second.

That is why the archive is full of hardware, and why the hardware is full of code. The interesting problems live in the seam between them.
