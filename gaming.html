👑 #1 ALL-TIME FAVORITE GAME HYPNOTIC DECKBUILDER & EXPONENTIAL MATH

BALATRO
ANTE 8 BOSS BLIND: DEFEATED.

Developed by LocalThunk (2024) · Published by Playstack. The poker roguelike that took over the world through pure mathematical dopamine, CRT phosphor bloom, and procedural Joker synergies.

“Play your cards right. Trigger the Jokers. Watch the numbers explode into scientific notation.”

Balatro is pure game design distilled to absolute perfection. It takes the universal 500-year-old vocabulary of poker hands — Pairs, Straights, Flushes, Full Houses — and violently re-engineers them with 150 unique Jokers that modify base chips and multipliers in real-time.

What makes it Debargha’s undisputed #1 favorite game is the tactile feedback loop: the satisfying clatter of cards flipping, the subtle screen tilt, the hypnotic analogue synth soundtrack composed by LouisF, and the euphoria when a Blueprint-Brainstorm combo triggers a Baron-Mime king re-trigger cascade, sending the mult counter spinning uncontrollably past 100,000,000.

THE CORONA QUARANTINE LIFELINE FRIENDSHIP IN ISOLATION

AMONG US
THERE IS 1 IMPOSTOR AMONG US.

InnerSloth (2018–2021). The social deduction phenomenon that saved our sanity during the Covid lockdowns. Late-night Discord calls, electrical room terrors, and emergency meetings that tested friendships.

WHO VENTED IN ELECTRICAL?
“Red was not an Anarchist. 1 Impostor remains.”

During the peak of the 2020–2021 quarantine, when physical classrooms went silent and the entire world was confined within four walls, Among Us wasn’t merely a game — it was our digital living room. Every evening at 9 PM, ten of us would jump into Discord, boot up The Skeld or Mira HQ, and argue passionately for three straight hours about who was fixing wires in Navigation and who faked card swipe in Admin.

The brilliant simplicity of its mechanic — asymmetric information, paranoia, body discovery panic, and vote-or-eject democracy — created legendary inside jokes that still persist among high school friends today. It demonstrated the profound power of multiplayer games to bridge distance and stave off loneliness when reality felt overwhelming.

// MEMORIES FROM ELECTRICAL

Fixing the circuit breaker in Electrical while checking your shoulder every two seconds; seeing the vent flap rattle; dead body reported; screaming defensive alibis while someone quietly chuckles in mute. It remains a core cultural milestone in Debargha’s gaming memory.

THE QUARANTINE BOND

Kept high school friends connected through laughter, accusations, and late-night voice chats.

PSYCHOLOGICAL ART

A masterclass in how human trust fractures and mends in micro-societies.

TOBY FOX'S MASTERWORK DETERMINATION & MERCY

UNDERTALE ×
DELTARUNE

Created by Toby Fox (2015 / 2018–Present). The retro pixel RPG that deconstructed gaming morality: showing mercy when combat was expected, and confronting the player directly with their own cruelty.

“Despite everything, it’s still you.”
— Undertale, Mirror in Asgore’s Home

Undertale is a landmark in interactive fiction because it remembered what standard RPGs ask you to forget: every enemy has a life, an insecurity, and a reason for being in the dark. The bullet-hell battle system turns dodging attacks into a dance of empathy where you can talk, spare, or befriend monsters rather than slaughtering them for EXP (Execution Points).

Deltarune deepens the existential inquiry: “In this world, your choices don’t matter... or do they?” Kris, Susie, and Ralsei exploring the Dark World with Toby Fox’s unmatched chiptune arrangements — like *MEGALOVANIA*, *Hopes and Dreams*, and *A Town Called Hometown* — cement Toby Fox as one of the greatest auteurs in modern gaming history.

DEV LOG // INDIE AUTHORSHIP SELF-DEVELOPED TITLES

SARBANASH ×
KISOR

Debargha Sarkar’s own indie game projects: a top-down pixel art Bengali survival horror game published on itch.io, and a narrative-driven visual novel. The seed of engineering that sparked everything.

PLAY SARBANASH ON ITCH.IO ↗ VIEW CODE DETAILS IN MAIN PORTFOLIO ↗
“Before I built quadruped robots or homelabs, I drew monsters pixel-by-pixel in the dead of night.”

Sarbanash was born out of a desire to create authentic psychological horror rooted in rural Bengal folk dread. Custom 16-bit sprite sheets, eerie ambient atmospheric loops, limited flashlight vision, and resource scarcity turned an amateur experiment into a fully realized playable game published on itch.io.

Paired with Kisor, a branching visual novel exploring memory, youth, and regret, game development was Debargha’s direct gateway into software engineering. Building game loops taught frame timing, 2D vector collisions, audio buffering, memory management, and sprite sheet caching — the very foundation that later enabled building 3D STL loaders, phone servers, and real-time robotic controllers.

// FROM SPRITES TO SYSTEMS

Game design forces an engineer to care about both extremes simultaneously: the lowest-level CPU cycles rendering a frame in 16.6ms, and the highest-level emotional empathy keeping a player immersed in a story. That duality informs every piece of code Debargha writes today.
