01
👑 #1 ALL-TIME FAVORITE SONG CROWN TRACK PITCH BLACK & BLOOD RED

MALICE MIZER
月下の夜想曲.

SONG: GEKKA NO YASOUKYOKU (NOCTURNE UNDER THE MOONLIGHT) RELEASE: 1998 · ALBUM: MERVEILLES LINEUP: MANA · GACKT · KÖZI · YŪKI · KAMI GENRE: FRENCH BAROQUE VISUAL KEI

My absolute number one favorite track in existence. A gothic marionette waltz beneath a blood-red moon. The sinister harpsichord arpeggios, the dollhouse rhythm, and Gackt's haunting theatrical baritone.

🥀 PICTURE: MALICE MIZER (HERO GESTURE) Place photo in: images/malice-mizer-hero.jpg
MANA & GACKT — THEATRICAL BAROQUE COSTUMES & GESTURES 1998 MERVEILLES ERA
🎭 PICTURE: DOLLHOUSE GESTURES & HARPSICHORD Place photo in: images/malice-mizer-gesture.jpg
MARIONETTE SYMBOLISM & GOTHIC GESTURES TOKYO NIPPON BUDOKAN
01 / THE HARPSICHORD CADENCE

The track opens with a chilling solo harpsichord cadence that sounds like an antique music box unearthed from an 18th-century French chateau. When the classical strings collide with the rock drum kit, the composition achieves pure dramatic majesty.

02 / WHY IT'S MY #1 CODING SOUNDTRACK

Most developers listen to generic elevator lo-fi beats. I put Gekka no Yasoukyoku on loop. The dark romantic precision keeps my mind locked into deep focus, whether I'm writing ESP32 C++ firmware, solving inverse kinematics, or compiling kernels in Termux.

02
ALT-ROCK & MELANCHOLIC CHIMES OK COMPUTER & PABLO HONEY

RADIOHEAD
CREEP & NO SURPRISES.

SONGS: CREEP (1992) · NO SURPRISES (1997) FRONTMAN: THOM YORKE SONIC MOOD: REZONANT GUITAR CRUNCH & GLOCKENSPIEL LULLABY

The ultimate sonic study in alienation. From Jonny Greenwood's brutal dead-string guitar chops right before the chorus in Creep, to the deceptively cheerful music-box chimes of No Surprises.

📻 PICTURE: RADIOHEAD / THOM YORKE Place photo in: images/radiohead.jpg
01 / "A HEART THAT'S FULL UP LIKE A LANDFILL"

No Surprises is the anthem of modern burnout — an anti-capitalist prayer packaged as a gentle children's lullaby. The glockenspiel riff is pure poetry.

FAVORITE LYRIC: "No alarms and no surprises, please."
03
ANALOG SYNTH & PSYCHEDELIC POP CURRENTS ERA

TAME IMPALA
THE LESS I KNOW
THE BETTER.

ARCHITECT: KEVIN PARKER (SOLO STUDIO MASTERMIND) SOUND: ROLAND JUNO-106 SYNTH · FUZZ BASS · PHASER DRUMS

Kevin Parker proved that one person alone in a bedroom studio can craft stadium-sized psychedelic disco. The infectious bassline of The Less I Know The Better is an instant adrenaline rush.

🌀 PICTURE: TAME IMPALA / CURRENTS Place photo in: images/tame-impala.jpg
01 / ONE-MAN STUDIO MASTERY

Kevin writes, records, performs, and produces every single note by himself. That solo DIY builder ethos resonates deeply with how I approach building hardware and codebases from scratch.

NEO-SOUL & EXPERIMENTAL HIP HOP IGOR & FLOWER BOY

TYLER, THE
CREATOR.

FAVORITE MOMENT: "CAN I GET A KISS?" (SEE YOU AGAIN) VIBE: WARM ANALOG PASTEL CHORDS & CREATIVE AUDACITY

Tyler's fearlessness in evolving from raw underground shock-rap to gorgeous neo-soul chords in Flower Boy and IGOR is pure inspiration.

05
RAW POST-PUNK & INDIE ROCK HIGH-CONTRAST B&W

ARCTIC
MONKEYS.

KEY TRACK: 505 (FAVOURITE WORST NIGHTMARE) PALETTE: STARK MONOCHROME BLACK & WHITE LEAD: ALEX TURNER

Pure high-contrast British rock. "I'm going back to 505 / If it's a seven hour flight or a forty-five minute drive..." The slow organ crescendo that erupts into thunderous drums and crashing guitars.

📼 PICTURE: ARCTIC MONKEYS (HIGH CONTRAST B&W) Place photo in: images/arctic-monkeys.jpg
ALEX TURNER // ANALOG OSCILLOSCOPE WAVEFORM AESTHETIC 505 FINALE
01 / THE ORGAN EXPLOSION AT 2:30

The tension in 505 builds on just two gentle organ chords sampled from Ennio Morricone. When the rhythm section detonates at 2:30, it delivers one of the most raw, visceral climaxes in indie rock history.
